Reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Photovania version, and what happened. Steps to reproduce the problem are especially helpful. Only attach screenshots or personal information you’re comfortable sharing by email.

Common questions

Where are my photos and notes stored?

Photovania stores its library locally on your device. There’s no Photovania account or app-managed cloud sync. Device backups depend on your Apple settings.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Taking photos, importing available local images, writing notes, and searching work offline. Downloading artwork from a web address requires a connection. Images stored only in iCloud Photos may need to download first.

How do I find text in a photo?

Open a game and use search. Photovania searches notes and text recognized in your saved photos. Recognition happens on-device; its accuracy depends on the image and how legible the text is.

Why isn’t the camera working?

Check Photovania’s camera permission in the Settings app. You can also import a screenshot from Photos instead.

How do I remove my data?

Delete individual captures or a game collection inside Photovania. Deleting a game also deletes its captures. Images you originally imported from Photos remain in your Photos library.

For details about data handling, read the Photovania privacy policy.
