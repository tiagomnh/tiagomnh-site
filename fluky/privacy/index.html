Fluky

Privacy policy

Fluky is developed by Tiago Henriques. This policy describes Fluky 2.0 for iPhone and iPad.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Choices, settings and results

Fluky stores your tool settings, custom Picker choices and the latest 50 results per tool locally on your device. No account is required. The app does not upload this information to a developer-operated server and does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

The five randomizers work offline. Random results are generated on your device.

Shake detection

While the 8 Ball screen is active, Fluky can use your device’s motion sensors to detect a shake and reveal an answer. Motion readings are processed on-device; the app does not save them or send them to a server. You can use the Ask button without shaking the device.

Analytics and advertising

Fluky 2.0 does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight and diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Support and privacy web pages

If you open a web page from About, Fluky loads it in an in-app browser. Website requests disclose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser information, to the website’s hosting provider.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. This website does not add analytics, advertising, forms or cookies. GitHub may process request information to operate and secure its hosting service under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following other links may open services with their own privacy policies.

Backups, retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you edit or clear it, it is replaced by newer history entries, or you delete the app. Each tool retains at most 50 results. Your local data may be included in device backups depending on your Apple settings.

You can clear a tool’s results from its history screen and edit or remove Picker choices in the app. Delete Fluky through iOS or iPadOS Settings to remove all of its local settings and data. Apple’s tools manage backups separately.

If you contact support

If you email support, I receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. I use that information to respond, investigate the issue and handle follow-up. Email providers process the correspondence under their own policies. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Contact me if you want support correspondence deleted or have questions about information you have shared.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if Fluky’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Tiago Henriques at tiagomnh@gmail.com.
