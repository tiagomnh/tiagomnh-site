Common questions

These instructions describe Fluky 2.0, which is currently in testing.

Does Fluky work offline?

Yes. Dice, Coin Flip, Random Number, Decision Picker and 8 Ball all work offline. Opening the support or privacy web pages requires an internet connection.

Where is my history?

Open a tool and tap the history button in its top-right corner. Each tool keeps its latest 50 results. Use Clear History in that view to remove that tool’s results.

How do I add choices to Picker?

Open Decision Picker and enter one choice per line. Tap Pick to choose one at random. You can edit or remove the choices in the same field.

Why can’t I generate unique numbers?

Your range must contain at least as many whole numbers as the count you request. Increase the range, lower Count or turn off No repeats.

How do I use the 8 Ball?

Open 8 Ball and tap Ask. You can also shake a supported iPhone while that screen is active. There is no question-entry field. If shaking does not trigger a result, use the Ask button.

Where are my choices and results stored?

They are saved locally on your device. Fluky does not require an account or provide its own cloud sync. Device backups depend on your Apple settings.

How do I delete my data?

Clear each tool’s history from its history screen and edit or remove your Picker choices in the app. To remove all local settings and data, delete Fluky through iOS or iPadOS Settings. Device backups are managed separately through Apple’s tools.

Read the Fluky privacy policy for details about data handling.
